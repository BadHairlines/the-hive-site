HOW TO JOIN

No direct-connect hassle. Find The Hive from DayZ on Xbox and favorite the map you play.

01 — Open Community

Launch DayZ on Xbox and open the Community tab in the server browser.

02 — Search The Hive

Search for 50x | THE HIVE and choose Chernarus or Livonia.

03 — Favorite It

Favorite the server so it is easy to find again after a restart or maintenance window.
